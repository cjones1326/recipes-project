A simple recipe for a grilled cheese sandwich.

Serves: 1 | Prep time: 3 minutes | Cook time: 8 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 slices of cheese
  • 1 tablespoon butter

Instructions

  1. Heat skillet to low heat.
  2. Put butter on one side of each slice of bread.
  3. Place one slice of bread with butter down in the skillet.
  4. Add cheese and place the second slice of bread on top.
  5. Cook over low-medium heat until the bottom is golden brown.
  6. Flip sandwich and cook until the othe side is golden brown and cheese is melted.
