Peanut Butter Sandwich

Home Resources Converter

A simple recipe for a peanut butter sandwich.

Serves: 1 | Prep time: 2-3 minutes | Cook time: None

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2-3 tablespoons peanut butter

Instructions

  1. Spread peanut butter on one slice of bread.
  2. Place the second slice of bread on top.
